About us

Wound healing where you feel most at home

PointCare Health Services is a mobile wound care practice serving Virginia, North Carolina, and South Carolina. Our clinicians treat chronic and hard-to-heal wounds wherever patients live: private homes, assisted living communities, and skilled nursing facilities.

Every patient gets a treatment plan built around their wound, their health, and their daily life, and we keep their physicians informed after every visit. Our goal is to heal the wound, keep it from coming back, and help patients get back to living.

What we do

Our Services

Complete wound care from experienced clinicians, delivered to your door.

1

Wound Assessment & Diagnosis

A full evaluation at the first visit. We identify the wound type, measure and stage it, and find what's slowing healing, such as diabetes, poor circulation, venous disease, or infection.

2

Infection Management

We check for and treat wound infections, order cultures and lab work, start antibiotics when they're needed, and track the response at every visit.

3

Compression Therapy & Edema Management

Multilayer compression wraps and swelling control for venous leg ulcers and swollen legs, improving blood flow so wounds can close.

4

Diabetes & Vascular-Related Wound Care

Treatment for diabetic foot ulcers, neuropathic and ischemic wounds, and circulation-related wounds, with offloading, blood sugar guidance, and vascular referrals when needed.

5

Advanced Wound Treatment

Evidence-based therapies including advanced dressings, debridement, compression, negative pressure wound therapy, ultrasound therapy, and skin substitutes to speed healing.

6

Patient Education & Prevention

Practical guidance for patients and caregivers on wound care at home, nutrition, diabetes control, and offloading, so wounds heal and stay healed.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you see me?

We usually schedule the first visit within 1–3 days of receiving a referral or your request.

What areas do you serve?

We serve patients across Virginia, North Carolina, and South Carolina, at home, in assisted living, and in skilled nursing facilities. Call us if you're not sure whether we cover your area.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

A physician referral helps, but you can contact us directly. We'll walk you through the process and coordinate with your doctor.

What types of wounds do you treat?

Pressure injuries, diabetic foot ulcers, venous and arterial ulcers, surgical wounds, traumatic wounds, and other chronic wounds that aren't healing.

Do you treat patients in nursing homes and assisted living?

Yes. We partner with skilled nursing facilities, assisted living communities, and home health agencies to provide wound care on-site.

Is wound care covered by insurance?

We accept Medicare Part B, Medicaid, and most major commercial plans, including Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, and Humana. Our intake team verifies your benefits before the first visit at no charge.
